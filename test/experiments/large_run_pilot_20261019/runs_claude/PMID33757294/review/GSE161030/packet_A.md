- `treatment_protocol_ch1`: C57BL/6 male mice (n=142, 8-10 weeks old and weighing 20-25 g) were provided by the Animal Center of Southern Medical University. The mice were divided into two groups, an exercise hypertrophic preconditioning (EHP; n=72) group and a sedentary control (Sed; n=70) group. The EHP group performed an endurance exercise protocol consisting of daily swimming training to induce physiological cardiac hypertrophy, according to a previously described protocol, with minor modifications. The swimming program started with 2 daily sessions of 15 minutes, and sessions were separated by at least 6 hours. The length of each session was then increased by 15 minutes every 2 days until they lasted 90 minutes; the program was conducted on 5 days/week, followed by 2 days of rest. The twice daily 90-minute swimming sessions were continued for the next 7 days. The protocol thus lasted for a total of 21 days. The mice were closely observed to avoid relative hypoxia. Mice in the Sed group were free to move around the cage. One week after the end of exercise training, all mice underwent either transverse aortic constriction (TAC) or a sham operation.
