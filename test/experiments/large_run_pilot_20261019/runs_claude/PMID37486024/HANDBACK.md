# HANDBACK — PMID 37486024 / GSE224900

**Status: finished** (decision `valid`, execution `completed`, validator 19 records / 0 errors).

## What ran
- Route: case spec (`case_spec.json`, generated by `prep/write_spec.py`), `python -m tools.case_runner execute`.
- Input prep (`prep/prep_merge.py`): merged the two deposited count files (19565R: 101 biopsies, 20063R: 12) on identical Ensembl IDs. Built the participant key `subject` (GEO has none) from sample-number blocks, each starting at a PREbc biopsy. Age, sex and treatment are constant within every block, which gives 9 placebo and 10 metformin participants.
- Activity estimand: bed-rest disuse (POST vs PRE) and 7-day re-ambulation (REC vs PRE), within each arm. There is no exercise-training arm. The placebo arm is primary because it isolates disuse and reloading. The metformin arm is secondary because drug exposure or withdrawal is mixed in.
- Samples: fasted PRE, POST and REC biopsies only (S3.4.p1). The clamp (PREac/POSTac) and Day1 biopsies were excluded.
- Model: DESeq2 (pydeseq2 Wald), `~ subject + time`, one model per arm (`run_joint_deseq2` with a subset per arm).
- Threshold: the paper's rule, padj < 0.05 with no fold-change cutoff (S2.7.p1, Fig 5 legend).
- Sensitivity: `~ time` without the subject term. Exploratory: per-sex fits within each arm.
- Enrichment: Hallmark GSEA (preranked on stat) on the REC tables of both arms. The paper ran fgsea on KEGG/Reactome at 10% FDR, so this run is a direction check only.

- The unpaired counts are much closer to the paper's for placebo. The paper's hciR model may not have blocked on participant, but the paper does not state its formula.
- The qualitative pattern matches the paper: the recovery response is larger than the bed-rest response, and it is larger in placebo than in metformin.
- Named genes in placebo REC vs PRE are all significantly up: COL1A1 log2FC 5.5, COL3A1 4.1, COL1A2 3.3, CDKN1A 2.2, TP53 0.8. CDKN1A and TP53 are also up at placebo POST. This agrees with S3.4.p1 and S3.5.p1.
- GSEA: EMT, inflammatory, TNFα/NF-κB and TGF-β gene sets are up, and OXPHOS is down, at REC in **both** arms with similar NES (EMT 2.74 in both).
  - The paper reports the collagen/TGF-β profile as blunted with metformin. Our per-arm GSEA does not show that blunting. The paper's GSEA was placebo-versus-metformin, which we did not run.
- Per-sex fits (exploratory): placebo F 1216/1152, placebo M 51/3465, metformin F 1/3, metformin M 440/3306 (POST/REC).

## Deviations
1. pydeseq2 was used instead of R DESeq2 1.30 with hciR, and our model blocks on participant. The paper does not state its formula, and the unpaired sensitivity suggests it may not have blocked.
2. Participant IDs were inferred from biopsy blocks rather than taken from GEO (medium confidence).
3. Sex labelling problems in the metformin arm, found by XIST and chrY expression across all 6 biopsies per block:
   - 19565_S084: the block is entirely female, but GEO says M. The block was kept and its label corrected in the `sex` column.
   - 19565X81 (S078 POSTbc) and 19565X96 (S096 PREbc): each is a single male biopsy in a female block. Both were excluded.
   - 19565_S090: PRE biopsies are female, but Day1/POST/REC are male. All its PRE/POST/REC biopsies were excluded.
   - Result: the metformin arm has PRE 8, POST 8, REC 9 samples. The paper presumably used the labels as deposited.
4. The placebo arm has 9 sequenced participants, while Table 1 lists 10 (Fig 5: N = 9–10).
5. No treatment × time interaction was run and no placebo-versus-metformin GSEA was run. Both answer the drug question, not the activity question.

## Tool problems
- `tools/stats_tools.py:257` `_detect_sex_column`, used by `tools/suitability.py:413,421`: the sex check picks the first sex-like metadata column. The spec has no field to name a corrected column. Even after relabelling, the check compared against `sex_geo_reported` and still lists the 3 S084 biopsies as mismatches (warning only, not blocking).
- The case runner cannot derive a participant key from sample-number blocks. Neither `patterns`, `combine` nor `recode` can express it, so a prep script had to add the `subject` column to the metadata.
- Not a tool bug: listing nested covariates (sex, run) in `design.covariate_candidates` makes the suitability design check rank-deficient, and the verdict becomes `not_analyzable`. Leaving them out fixed this. The resulting message ("failed: covariance") does not say that nesting within subject was the cause.
- The Bash heredoc for the spec writer failed on a regex with quotes. That is an environment problem; the file was written with the Write tool instead.
