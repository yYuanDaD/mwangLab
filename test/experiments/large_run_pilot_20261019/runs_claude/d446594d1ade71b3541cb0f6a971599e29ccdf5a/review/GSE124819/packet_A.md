Animals were anaesthetized with pentobarbital (50 mg/kg, IP) and given prophylactic antibiotics (gentamicin sulphate, 15 mg/kg, SC) prior to surgery and again 3 and 5 days post-injury. After recovery, animals in the SCI Cage Size groups were housed two/cage in standard cages (22鈥 x 12.5鈥 x 8鈥), tiny cages (7.5鈥 x 8.5鈥 x 8鈥), to restrict their movement, or large cages (16鈥 x 20鈥 x 8鈥 ), to allow for greater movement for the duration of the study. The subjects were randomized into three groups: No SCI+Standard, CONT SCI+Tiny, and CONT SCI+Large. For animals in the SCI+Activity groups, this involved housing animals two/cage in standard cages (22鈥 x 12.5鈥 x 8鈥) or tiny cages (7.5鈥 x 8.5鈥 x 8鈥, to restrict their activity) for the duration of the study. The subjects were randomized into five groups: No SCI, TX SCI, CONT SCI, CONT SCI + swimming (SWIM), and CONT SCI + shallow water walking (SWW).  Swimming and shallow water walking (three five minute bouts separated by a short break twice/day) began 10 days post-injury and was conducted 5 consecutive days/week for 8 weeks.
