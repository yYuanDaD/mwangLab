# Paper workflow safety

Apply this checklist before selecting tools or presenting a computational plan.

1. Build a dataset inventory from the target paper: accession, organism, modality, role, and whether the dataset was generated by the paper or only cited.
2. Keep different datasets and modalities separate until their sample identities, matrix scales, and biological roles are established.
3. For single-cell data, require biological sample/subject identifiers before pseudobulk. Never treat cells as biological replicates.
4. Do not manufacture a treatment-control contrast for observational, clustering, prognostic, or external-validation designs. Mark the design for manual review when no defensible two-arm contrast exists.
5. Choose DA methods from the actual numeric matrix type:
   - Raw integer counts may use DESeq2, edgeR, or limma-voom.
   - FPKM/TPM, already log-transformed expression, microarray intensity, and processed proteomics must use the project's plain `run_limma_analysis` path.
   - Never use DESeq2 or limma-voom on FPKM/TPM or already log-transformed values.
6. Keep paper-reported claims distinct from newly computed results and attach evidence to both.
7. Copy no accession, group, method, or conclusion from the bundled example unless it independently occurs in the target paper.

Use the bundled example as a reasoning demonstration, not as evidence about the target study.
